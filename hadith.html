Daily Hadith

Take a moment to read, reflect, and learn from the beautiful teachings of the Prophet ﷺ:

  • "You will do everything best. This is Allah's order." - Prophet (PBUH)
  • "Every morning is a transitional period in front of people. Two paths lie before him. (When he surrenders his actions to the thought of the Day of Judgment, he) advances on the path of salvation or (when he enslaves instincts,) he brings about his own destruction." - Abu Malek Ash'ari (RA); Muslim, Prophet
  • "Allah will not look at your appearance or wealth, He will look at your heart and take account of your deeds." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim, Ibn Majah
  • "Allah will not give any reward on the Day of Great Judgment for the good deeds done to show people or to get people's praise." - Ubai Ibn Ka'b (RA); Ahmad
  • "He who wants to be great by developing qualities, Allah makes him great. And whoever wants consumer goods and wealth, Allah gives them to him." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Bokhari
  • "People will rise according to their destiny." - Abu Hurairah (RA), Jabir (RA); Ibn Majah
  • "Whoever changes his perspective, Allah will change his surroundings." - Abdullah Ibn Amr (RA); Hakem
  • "Never think that I can't do the job. Do that which is good for you, and seek Allah's help." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim
  • "Those who consider danger to be a misfortune, those who consider it a blessing to be easily accomplished, will never be among the honorable pioneers." - Abdullah Ibn Abbas (RA); Tabrani
  • "I am not something extra human. When I give any instruction about religion or life, follow it completely. And when it comes to worldly matters, I am one like you." - Rafi Ibn Khadij (RA); Muslim, Meshkat
  • "Acquiring knowledge is obligatory for every believer." - Anas Ibn Malek (RA); Nasa'i, Meshkat
  • "The wise are those who act according to knowledge." - Kab (RA); Meshkat
  • "Real truth is your duty to tell others what you know. And if you do not know anything about something, you should say 'Allah knows best about it'. Because to be able to say 'Allah knows best' about what people have no knowledge about is also knowledge. (One should never pretend to know or make false claims)" - Mashrooq (RA); Bokhari
  • "Whoever walks on the path of truth, Allah makes the path to Paradise easy for him." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim
  • "If you find a man who does not gossip and abstains from worldly pleasures, take his company. (For from him you will find wisdom.)" - Abu Hurairah (RA), Abu Khallad (RA); Bayhaqi, Ibn Majah
  • "Wisdom begins at a higher level of creator-consciousness." - Abdullah Ibn Masood (RA); Hakem
  • "Congratulations to the one who embraces Islam, who has the necessities of life and is content with what Allah has given him. He is successful." - Fadala Ibn Ubayd (RA), Abdullah Ibn Amr (RA); Tirmidhi, Muslim
  • "Express gratitude. He who does not show gratitude to the people who have benefited, can never be a slave of Allah." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Abu Dawood, Ahmad
  • "Instead of looking at someone who is richer than you or more beautiful than you, look at someone who is poorer than you or less beautiful than you. Only then will you realize the grace of your Lord upon you." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Bokhari, Muslim
  • "At the death of the ungrateful servant, when his soul is told, Come forth! Then he said, I will not come willingly, if I want to come I will come as an invalid." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Bokhari, Tirmidhi
  • "If you are a believer, speak well or be silent." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim
  • "By thinking that one speaks for the welfare of others, Hell is removed by Allah far away from him." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Bokhari, Muslim
  • "A true believer can never be sarcastic, cursing, abusive and abusive." - Abdullah Ibn Masood (RA); Tirmidhi, Hakem
  • "It is a great sin to give two insults in response to one insult." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Abu Dawood
  • "Do not abuse each other. If the second person does not transgress in response to the abuse, the person who gave the first abuse will be held guilty." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
  • "Whoever among you has the best character is the best as a person." - Abdullah Ibn Amr (RA); Bokhari, Muslim
  • "Good people are good people who are virtuous or virtuous and have a long life." - Abdullah Ibn Bushar (RA); Tirmidhi
  • "You cannot satisfy everyone with wealth. Satisfy people with smiling faces and good behavior." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Hakem, Ashkalani
  • "A believer's most valuable virtue on the Day of Judgment will be his good conduct." - Abu Darda (RA); Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
  • "Allah is Most Merciful. He loves harmony. What harmony can achieve, rigor never can. He favors like-minded people." - Ayesha (RA); Muslim
  • "He who does not have compassion for others has no virtue." - Jarir Ibn Abdullah (RA); Muslim
  • "Sincere humility is the source of all virtues." - Meshkat
  • "Modesty and gentle behavior are part of Sadaka." - Jabir Ibn Abdullah (RA); Ibn Hibban
  • "Allah loves patience and humility." - Abdullah Ibn Abbas (RA); Muslim
  • "Modesty never diminishes respect. When one humbles himself for the sake of Allah, Allah surely increases his honor." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Muslim
  • "Allah has mercy on the merciful. So be kind to the living, Allah will be kind to you." - Abdullah Ibn Amr (RA); Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi
  • "Surely Allah is gentle. He likes tenderness. What He gives because of the tenderness of His servants, He does not give to those who are harsh." - Abdullah Ibn Mugaffal (RA); Mufrad (Bokhari), Muslim
  • "Forgive others. You will receive Allah's forgiveness." - Abdullah Ibn Amr (RA); Bokhari, Abu Dawood
  • "He who forgives, despite the power to take revenge, will be more honored by Allah." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Bayhaqi, Ibn Hibban
  • "Think of death often. Then worldly greed and addiction will decrease. Controlling biological urges will be easier." - Abu Hurairah (RA); Tirmidhi, Nasa'i
  • "Keep your tongue under control. Many people will be thrown into hell because of the tongue." - Mu'az Ibn Jabal (RA); Tirmidhi
  • "Whoever wants to be patient, Allah gives him enough patience. Patience is Allah's greatest gift to His servant." - Abu Saeed Khudri (RA); Bokhari, Muslim
